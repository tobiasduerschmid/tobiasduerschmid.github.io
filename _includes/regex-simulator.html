Runtime and result limits

Pattern execution stops after 2 seconds. Stop cancels a run or simulator preparation. Your inputs stay available for another attempt.

Results display up to 200 matches and 100 candidate starting positions. To keep the page responsive, a run can return at most 5,000 capture records and 100,000 characters of match and capture text. Substitution output is limited to 100,000 characters. If a result exceeds these limits, try shorter text or fewer capture groups.
