{%- comment -%} The control bar of every narration player. It is the song player's bar (_layouts/song-player.html) with the narration's choices: the same icons (player-icon.html) and layout, with the position line above playback on the left and the view on the right, and the same settings panel, which floats over the player (js/song-video/settings-panel.js). Its song-player class hooks give every player the song player's keyboard shortcuts and full-screen behavior (js/narration-player-controls.js). Params: id prefix of the ids this bar refers to (captions, settings) text_only true for a story without rendered speech: no transport, position, volume, captions toggle, or speed duration the narration's length in seconds seek_label the position slider's accessible name scenes the story's scenes, for the scene select scene_label the scene select's label (default "Scene") speed_label see narration-player-speed.html lead controls before the transport, such as a case study's steps view choices that stay in the bar, before the view controls, such as a lesson's code language settings settings that only this kind of player offers {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign controls_duration = include.duration | default: 0 | floor -%} {%- assign controls_seconds = controls_duration | modulo: 60 -%}
