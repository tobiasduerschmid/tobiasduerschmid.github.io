Playback tips and shortcuts {%- if include.text_only %}

With focus on the player or its controls, F toggles full screen, and Esc leaves it.

{%- else %} {%- if include.picture %}

Click or tap the picture to play or pause. Double-click or double-tap its left half to go back 5 seconds, or its right half to go forward 5 seconds. In full screen, the controls hide after 3 seconds of playback; move the pointer, press a key, or tap the picture to show them.

{%- else %}

In full screen, the controls hide after 3 seconds of playback; move the pointer or press a key to show them.

{%- endif %}

With focus on the {% if include.picture %}picture{% else %}player{% endif %} or the controls: K or Space plays or pauses, ← and → go back or forward 5 seconds, 0 to 9 jump to the start or 10% to 90% of the narration, C turns captions on or off, and F toggles full screen.
