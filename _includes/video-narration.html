Playback tips and shortcuts

Click or tap the picture to play or pause. Double-click or double-tap its left half to go back 5 seconds, or its right half to go forward 5 seconds. In full screen, the controls hide after 3 seconds of playback; move the pointer, press a key, or tap the picture to show them.

With focus on the picture or the controls: K or Space plays or pauses, ← and → go back or forward 5 seconds, 0 to 9 jump to the start or 10% to 90% of the narration, C turns captions on or off, and F toggles full screen. Esc leaves full screen. The narration pauses for a knowledge check at the end of each section.
