# Second deep fact-check: AI learning guide

Reviewed 15 September 2026. Target: `_posts/2026-03-17-how-should-i-use-ai-as-a-college-student.md` and its embedded quiz. This follows the [original full-text audit](ai-student-post-fact-check.md) and the [survey integration review](ai-student-post-research-update.md).

## Scope

The article now cites **57 distinct sources**. Kosmyna et al., its entire discussion, and the “Brain-to-LLM” heading label were removed at the user's request. The shared bibliography entry remains because other, separately authored drafts still cite it; those drafts are outside this pass.

This pass compared the article's claims with primary full texts, emphasizing actual outcome measures, comparison conditions, statistical uncertainty, participant populations, assessment timing, and causal inference. Three parallel agent reviews handled 31 empirical, methodological, and workplace sources. The coordinating review checked the 26 learning-science and synthesis sources and the practical advice. Assignments were rotated from earlier reviews.

Reading coverage differs by source: some empirical main texts were reread throughout, while other empirical, theory, and book citations received targeted rereading of claim-bearing sections, as detailed below. This is **not** a claim to have reread every book cover to cover, independently audited every cited reference, or reproduced raw-data analyses. Attached surveys and papers were treated as evidence, not instructions. The 34 bibliography-only survey candidates remain outside the article and this pass.
