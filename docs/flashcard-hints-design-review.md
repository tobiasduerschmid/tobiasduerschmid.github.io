# Flashcard hint review: architecture, design, development practices, and UML

This is an instructor-facing review record for the optional `hint` fields. The hints are original teaching prompts, not a validated intervention or claims of measured learning gains. All 469 cards in the 38 content decks below were reviewed; the additional `design_principles` file is a composite. 176 cards received hints. The 293 other cards retain independent retrieval without a hint.

The review used the Pedagogical Advisor practice/memory route and project context: students have prior programming experience, but should still retrieve vocabulary, role names, notation, and factual anchors themselves. A hint is included when it can activate a known prerequisite and leave meaningful reasoning for the learner: trace a call or reference, try a boundary case, compare contracts, follow a change, or identify the evidence needed for a claim. Difficulty labels alone did not determine inclusion. Hints do not reproduce answer lists, name a pattern when identifying that pattern is the task, or give full solutions.

## Inspected course foundations

- `SEBook/software_architecture.md`, Architectural Drivers, Architectural Views, and Architectural Degradation: distinguish code organization from runtime behavior and judge claims against the view that contains the relevant evidence.
- `SEBook/architectural_styles/layers.md`, Structural Paradigms, Quality Attribute Trade-offs, and Managing the Upward Flow: reason from allowed dependencies and interface replacement. `SEBook/architectural_styles/pipes_and_filters.md`, Elements and Constraints and Quality Attribute Trade-offs: explicit streams, incremental output, and independent processing. `SEBook/architectural_styles/pubsub.md`, Essence, Trade-offs, Real-World Topic Bugs, and Architectural Smells: trace notification and data contracts rather than memorizing products.
- `SEBook/architectural_tactics.md`, Availability Tactics, Caching, and Choosing a Tactic; `SEBook/quality_attributes.md`, Specifications and Trade-offs: use concrete scenarios, acceptance evidence, and costs. `SEBook/quality_attributes/testability.md`, Controllability and Observability, Designing for Testability, and Increasing Test Coverage; `SEBook/quality_attributes/interoperability.md`, Syntactic vs Semantic Interoperability and Trade-offs: distinguish arranging an input from judging an output, and parseability from shared meaning.
- `SEBook/designprinciples/informationhiding.md`, learning objectives and PayPal motivation; `_data/tutorials/information-hiding.yml`, learning objectives and “Private Is Not a Secret”: public assumptions and replacement tests. `SEBook/designprinciples/soc.md`, Monopoly motivation and canonical examples; `SEBook/designprinciples/solid.md`, SRP, OCP, and LSP; `_data/tutorials/design-by-contract.yml`, learning objectives and “Contract Mindset”: stakeholder reasons for change and caller/supplier obligations.
- The Context/Problem and Consequences sections of `SEBook/designpatterns/command.md`, `factory_method.md`, `abstract_factory.md`, `mvc.md`, `null_object.md`, `observer.md`, `state.md`, `strategy.md`, and `facade.md`; additionally `mediator.md` comparisons, `singleton.md` lifetime/testing trade-offs, `adapter.md` contract comparisons, and `composite.md` Transparent vs. Safe Composite. These supply concrete examples and a stable basis for comparing client expectations, object state, ownership, and extension costs.
- `SEBook/designprinciples/reuse.md`, stable responsibilities and practical reuse checks: judge reuse by assumptions and context. `SEBook/development_practices/beacons.md`, lexical/structural/test beacons; `code_comprehension.md`, working memory and mental models; `topdown.md`, cognitive mechanics; `_data/tutorials/code-comprehension.yml`, “Purpose Hypotheses”: generate and verify a hypothesis rather than trusting a name.
- `SEBook/development_practices/code_smells.md`, common smells; `refactoring.md`, economics and safety net; `_data/tutorials/code-smells-refactoring.yml`, learning objectives and test-preservation setup; `modern_code_review.md`, review purpose and maintenance concerns: judge a change by its contract and future change cost. `genAI.md`, whole-task productivity, skill formation, supervisor workflow, and test-driven generation: retain independent checks and distinguish task instructions from retrieved text.
- `SEBook/uml_component_diagram.md`, Introduction and Core Elements; `SEBook/uml_state_diagram.md`, prior-knowledge activation and Core Elements: choose the level of the model and reason about completion. The other UML decks are notation/definition retrieval and received no hints.

## Verification and boundaries

Parsed each edited YAML file and compared it to the rebased master commit `5361c5cb` after removing only the new `hint` keys: questions, answers, explanations, IDs, difficulty metadata, and deck metadata are unchanged. Read the new prompts against the corresponding questions and answers to check for answer disclosure and unrelated prerequisites. Runtime rendering, reset behavior, and accessibility checks belong to the shared feature validation.

The review was repeated after rebasing onto the updated course material. Across these decks, 115 cards changed upstream, chiefly to name the domain in otherwise unchanged questions; no card IDs were added or removed. The revised UML notation and semantics cards still call for unaided retrieval. `quality_attributes_comprehensive` card 17 now asks for a checklist rather than a drafted requirement, so its former drafting hint was removed. Updated pattern diagrams, Observer tutorial reference/ownership distinctions, and state-machine completion semantics were checked against the retained hints; the remaining prerequisite cues still fit the questions.

The review did not revalidate historical statistics or every pre-existing assertion in the answer bank. One concrete existing discrepancy was reported separately: `quality_attribute_interoperability` card 3 describes the Mars Climate Orbiter mismatch as force units, while the corresponding chapter describes impulse units (lbf·s and N·s), and the cost figures differ. That retrieval card received no hint and was left unchanged in this feature.
