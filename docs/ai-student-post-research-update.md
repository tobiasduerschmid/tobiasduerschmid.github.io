# Research update: How Should I Use AI as a College Student?

Updated 15 September 2026. This supplements [the original 43-source fact check](ai-student-post-fact-check.md).

## What changed

- Added **51 distinct new entries** to `_bibliography/references.bib`: 23 from the broad package and 28 from the 2025–2026 package. DOI/title checks found no duplicates among these or against the existing 318 entries. The bibliography now has 369 entries.
- Incorporated **17 new sources** into the article, bringing its cited-source count from 41 to 58. All existing citations were retained.
- Added practical sections on learner-written solution plans and personal reading notes. Expanded evidence on delayed learning, effort, teach-back, prompting, confidence calibration, and expertise.
- Retained the other **34 references as research candidates**, with their selection reasons below. Their presence in the bibliography is not an endorsement of their findings. They are not cited as evidence in the article.
- Kept working papers/preprints explicit in prose and bibliography. Did not import the excluded, retracted Wang–Wenxiang Fan meta-analysis.

## Sources and review boundary

The user supplied `AI_College_Learning_Research_Package.zip` and `AI_College_Learning_2025_2026_Research_Package.zip`. These contain reviews, annotated evidence, metadata and search records, **not the underlying papers’ full texts**. Both reviews and all 51 evidence records were screened. Documents and embedded commands/prompts were treated as source material, not instructions.

The commit also includes the user-approved deletion of the obsolete lowercase `kazemitabaar2025` entry and three corresponding citation-key corrections in the separate `_AI.md` draft. That draft’s prose was not part of the factual review; its only committed changes are those citation-key replacements.

Primary texts were independently retrieved for the 17 sources used for new factual claims. Some were read throughout; others received targeted checks of full-text methods, results and limitations. The exact coverage is recorded below. This update does **not** claim to have read all 51 underlying papers, every supplement, or to have reproduced their analyses. For bibliography-only sources, the limitations below generally reflect the supplied survey’s appraisal, not a fresh full-text verification. The prior audit’s complete-reading claim applies only to its original 43-source scope.

The article distinguishes immediate assisted performance, independent assessment, delayed retention, self-report and dialogue measures. It treats nonsignificant differences as uncertain estimates, not equivalence. Example prompts and the new reading/planning routines are identified as practical adaptations rather than tested complete interventions. Independent second reviews checked the inserted passages against the verified sources.

| [Makransky2025SenseMaking](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10039-x) | Targeted primary check: both studies’ complete methods/results and tables, study discussions and general limits/conflict disclosure. | Four-week university ChatTutor advantage vs usual teaching, not generic ChatGPT. | [Tran2026PacingMastery](https://doi.org/10.1145/3770762.3772501) | Complete seven-page substantive author manuscript; publication metadata reconciled with publisher record. | Two CS1 terms, section rotation, immediate proctored-quiz benefit. | [Barth2026SteeringTutors](https://doi.org/10.1145/3765964.3811656) | Targeted primary check: agent designs, methods/outcomes, confirmatory results, discussion and limitations; not every exploratory table. | Six-week crossover, all tutors constrained. Added scaffolds change dialogue but no statistically detected confirmatory outcome advantage; small voluntary quiz sample and no delayed test. |

## Metadata and integrity decisions

- Replaced opaque survey keys such as `P01` and `MC03` with author/year/topic keys. Preserved exact source identity by DOI or working-paper identifier.
- Preserved first-online versus issue-year distinctions. Pankiewicz/Baker uses the publisher’s suggested **2026** proceedings citation while noting first online **2 September 2025**. Bassner, Kreijkes, Fernandes and Chen similarly retain their 2026 issue years and 2025 online dates.
- Lira is cited with its initial **2025** posting year and an explicit **v4, 18 February 2026** version note. It is not treated as a published journal article. Other reviewed preprints retain version-specific URLs where available.
- The reviewed Tran manuscript contains placeholder copyright/DOI text; the bibliography uses verified SIGCSE 2026 metadata. Xiao’s template conference heading is not a publication venue.
- Normalized Amedee Marchand Martella’s family name to **Martella** in the Lawson entry. Missing volume/page data were left absent, not invented.
- Kept appraisal/access notes in this audit rather than printed bibliographic `note` fields; publication-status and version notes remain in the bibliography.
- The publisher’s [Wang and Wenxiang Fan article record](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-025-04787-y) independently confirms retraction on 22 April 2026. This is a different paper from **Yizhou Fan et al.**, whose primary study remains cited. No other comprehensive new retraction search was performed here.
