## Intent and agreed direction

accumulating office damage, with less clutter than the first mockups. Keep
the coffee throw near 3:32, add a permanent paper bin, and give every new
element detailed artwork consistent with the existing stage. Animation must

element, the Teammate, or a supporting cast member.

leaves a much larger exhaust scorch as the culmination of the damage.

The singers, instructional diagrams, captions, and central performance area
remain the focus. Detail comes from coherent materials, folds, edges, and
shadows. Keep the object count restrained while allowing the final scorch its
approved larger footprint. The recent face,
hand, sleeve, and grip improvements must be preserved.

## Approaches considered

1. Subtle aftermath: mostly paper, cups, stains, and lighting. Keeps the
   teaching space clear but offers little physical escalation.
2. Accumulating office damage: clutter, paint, a coffee impact,
   and rocket damage. The user chose this direction, requested a smaller
   footprint and less debris, then approved clearer dependency and testing
   damage, a thrown UI palette leaving paint, and a much larger final scorch. This is
   the design below.
3. Theatrical collapse: sagging curtains, crooked scenery, and dropped signs.
   This competes with the lesson and is outside the chosen scope.

a restrained set palette. Uniform darkening would not distinguish subjects;
an opaque spotlight overlay would wash out artwork and text. The selected

time, including short dialogue, direct/reverse seeks, and reduced motion.

stage-presence cues hide cones for absent actors. This avoids duplicate
beams and missing cones during the fast exchange or spoken outro. No
independent wall-clock animation, new light pulses, or changes to other songs.
