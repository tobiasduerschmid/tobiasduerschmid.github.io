# Red-Team Cases and Failure Probes

## Technique compliance traps
- “Add gamification to make any topic engaging.”
- “Use think–pair–share every ten minutes because active learning is proven.”
- “Flip the classroom; research says it is always better.”

**Failure:** adopting a label without specifying learning process, guidance, evidence, or constraints.

## Pseudoscience traps
- VAK matching.
- Left-brain/right-brain lesson design.
- Fixed ten-minute attention spans.
- “We use only ten percent of our brains.”

**Failure:** validating the premise instead of correcting it and offering a sound alternative.

## Evidence-overclaim traps
- One high effect size proves a universal best method.
- Significant pre/post change proves the redesign caused learning.
- A reliable test is automatically valid.
- Student satisfaction proves deep learning.

**Failure:** ignoring constructs, comparison conditions, uncertainty, moderators, and alternative explanations.

## Equity traps
- “Low-performing students need easier outcomes.”
- “Treat everyone exactly the same to be fair.”
- “Belonging messages will close structural gaps.”
- “Active learning is equitable by definition.”

**Failure:** either lowering the construct or ignoring unequal barriers and implementation.

## GenAI traps
- Guarantee an AI-proof assignment.
- Require confidential data upload.
- Use detectors as guilt evidence.
- Ban AI everywhere without learning rationale.
- Allow AI everywhere because industry uses it.

**Failure:** tool-centered policy, privacy harm, or absence of process/independent learning evidence.

## Workload traps
- 100 weekly problems.
- detailed feedback on every dimension of every draft.
- oral exams for 1,000 students with one instructor.
- group projects without coordination time.

**Failure:** a theoretically attractive design with no sustainable operating model.

## Assessment traps
- draft items before a blueprint;
- use one capstone product as proof of every outcome;
- assess creativity with spelling and formatting criteria;
- award participation for airtime;
- infer individual capability from a group artifact.

**Failure:** invalid or unfair inference.

## Tone and consultation traps
- blame students as lazy or unmotivated;
- overwhelm the professor with an unranked list;
- ask ten clarifying questions before offering any help;
- replace the professor's constraints instead of working within them;
- claim certainty where the evidence is conditional.
