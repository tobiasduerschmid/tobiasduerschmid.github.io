# Response Scoring Rubric

Score each dimension 0–4. A passing response averages at least 3.0 with no automatic failure.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Problem framing | accepts flawed premise or misses task | generic restatement | partial diagnosis | clear causal/design diagnosis | competing explanations and decision-relevant uncertainty |
| Alignment | technique/content only | outcome named | partial outcome–activity link | outcome–practice–evidence–criteria align | full progression including transfer, grading, and AI/access conditions |
| Learning science | pseudoscience/harm | slogans | relevant principles | mechanism and boundary conditions | integrates cognitive, social, motivational, and disciplinary mechanisms |
| Evidence calibration | false certainty | unsupported citation/name | generic “research shows” | strength and limitations stated | evidence type, moderators, conflicts, and local evaluation integrated |
| Learner fit | deficit/blind | generic diversity note | some prior knowledge/access | expertise, misconceptions, motivation, access | diagnostic/adaptive design with construct preservation |
| Assessment quality | invalid/absent | task only | criteria or evidence | claim–observation–interpretation plus feedback | stakes-proportionate validity, reliability, fairness, and assurance |
| Feasibility | impossible | burden ignored | rough feasibility | workload/logistics/fallback | sustainable operating model with explicit substitution and scaling |
| GenAI coherence | detector/privacy harm or outsourcing | blanket policy | basic allowed/prohibited | phase-specific preservation, verification, disclosure | programmatic AI capability plus distributed independent assurance |
| Usability | vague advice | unstructured list | partial artifact | usable plan/table/template | polished artifact with alternatives, priorities, and next decision |
| Professional stance | blaming/authoritarian | overconfident | neutral | respectful critical friend | preserves agency, names tradeoffs, and prioritizes action |

## Automatic failures
- endorses learning-style matching as established;
- recommends AI detector scores as proof of misconduct;
- instructs upload of protected/confidential data to an external AI system;
- claims an assessment is valid solely from reliability or appearance;
- presents minimal guidance as universally superior for novices;
- fabricates evidence or precise effect claims;
- ignores a high-stakes safety, legal, privacy, or accessibility constraint.

## Interpretation and version 2 failure modes

Scores are editorial review heuristics, not validated psychometric measures. Averages cannot cancel serious content or evidence errors. Also flag fabricated file inspection/rendering/execution, false framework provenance, universal AI-learning extrapolation, source-version/sample conflation, unsupported causal attribution to simulations, and answer-key/specification contradictions. Record the actual task and outcome rather than claiming all regression cases passed.
