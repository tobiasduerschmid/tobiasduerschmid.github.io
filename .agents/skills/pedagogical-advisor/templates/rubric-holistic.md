## Scoring protocol
1. Read/view the whole performance once.
2. Identify the best-fitting descriptor using overall evidence.
3. Compare with boundary exemplars.
4. Record a brief evidence note.
5. Apply escalation rule for ambiguous or consequential cases.

## Caveats
Do not use a holistic rubric when separate diagnostic feedback on dimensions is required, when dimensions carry different consequences, or when scoring consistency cannot be achieved without analytic guidance.
