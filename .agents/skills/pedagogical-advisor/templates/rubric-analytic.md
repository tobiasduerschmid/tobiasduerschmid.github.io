## Design rules
- Criteria describe distinct dimensions of the target capability.
- Level descriptors name observable qualities, not vague adjectives alone.
- The top level is not perfection; it is a defensible performance standard.
- Avoid double- or triple-barreled criteria.
- Include only dimensions that were taught or are legitimate prerequisites.
- Weighting reflects importance rather than ease of scoring.
- Mechanics are separated from disciplinary reasoning unless both are central.
