# Syllabus and Course-Communication Audit

Rate each item: 0 absent, 1 unclear/incomplete, 2 adequate, 3 exemplary.

## Purpose and alignment
- [ ] Course purpose is intelligible to students.
- [ ] Outcomes describe observable capabilities.
- [ ] Assessment table shows how outcomes are evidenced.
- [ ] Calendar supports a coherent progression rather than a topic list only.

## Transparency and navigation
- [ ] Required actions, dates, locations, and submission routes are easy to find.
- [ ] Terminology is defined and consistent.
- [ ] Workload and preparation expectations are credible.
- [ ] Examples/checklists clarify complex tasks.

## Learning and feedback
- [ ] Students understand why retrieval, active learning, collaboration, or revision are used.
- [ ] Formative feedback and revision opportunities are visible.
- [ ] Help pathways and office-hour purposes are explicit.
- [ ] Late/recovery policies support learning while remaining manageable.

## Assessment and grading
- [ ] Criteria and weighting reflect course priorities.
- [ ] Grade calculation is reproducible from the syllabus.
- [ ] Collaboration and resource rules are task-specific.
- [ ] Regrade/appeal and moderation procedures are clear.

## Inclusion and access
- [ ] Materials and core communication are accessible.
- [ ] Accommodation process is welcoming and practical.
- [ ] Participation is not equated solely with spontaneous public speech.
- [ ] Language and examples avoid unnecessary exclusion.
- [ ] Cost, device, time-zone, and connectivity assumptions are addressed.

## Climate and belonging
- [ ] Norms communicate respect, high standards, and support.
- [ ] Errors, questions, and help seeking are normalized.
- [ ] Instructor commitments are stated alongside student responsibilities.
- [ ] Sensitive discussion procedures are specified where needed.

## GenAI and academic integrity
- [ ] AI rules are specific by task or phase.
- [ ] The rationale connects to learning outcomes.
- [ ] Disclosure, verification, privacy, and citation expectations are explicit.
- [ ] Independent-assurance points are identified.
- [ ] Policy does not rely on detector scores as proof.
