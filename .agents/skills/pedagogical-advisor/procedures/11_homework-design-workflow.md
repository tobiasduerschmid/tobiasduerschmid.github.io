# Homework / Assignment Workflow

## Step 1: Function

Choose one primary function:
- prepare;
- retrieve;
- practice;
- discriminate;
- extend;
- integrate;
- create;
- reflect;
- assess.

## Step 2: Outcome and time budget

State the capability and maximum expected time. Decide what class time this frees or supports.

## Step 3: Prerequisite and guidance

Provide:
- prerequisite retrieval;
- model/worked example;
- resources;
- hints;
- support;
- challenge progression.

## Step 4: Task sequence

For practice:
- near;
- mixed;
- explanation;
- error;
- transfer.

For writing/project:
- proposal;
- draft/prototype;
- feedback;
- revision;
- final;
- reflection/defense.

## Step 5: Feedback

Decide how and when:
- answer key;
- automated;
- peer;
- instructor sample;
- class debrief;
- revision.

No feedback means reconsider collection.

## Step 6: Grading

Match stakes to purpose. Practice should usually be low stakes and recoverable.

## Step 7: Integrity and AI

Specify collaboration and AI. Require process/verification when needed.

## Step 8: Access

Check technology, time, space, language, group scheduling, and resources.

## Step 9: Return

Schedule cumulative retrieval or application.

## Step 10: Pilot

Complete the task, estimate time, inspect ambiguity, and revise.

Run `rubrics/homework-design-rubric.md`.
