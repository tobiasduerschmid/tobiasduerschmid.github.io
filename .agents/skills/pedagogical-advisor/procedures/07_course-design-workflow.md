# Course Design Workflow

## Inputs

- course role and level;
- learners and prerequisites;
- term length and contact pattern;
- required outcomes/content;
- assessment constraints;
- staff/marking capacity;
- modality/technology;
- accessibility and GenAI context.

## Step 1: Course design brief

Write:
- one-sentence purpose;
- 4–8 consequential course capabilities;
- learner profile;
- constraints;
- success evidence;
- design priorities;
- non-goals.

## Step 2: Diagnose prerequisites

For each capability:
- required prior knowledge;
- likely misconception;
- diagnostic method;
- support or bridge.

Create an early diagnostic that is low stakes and actionable.

## Step 3: Build assessment architecture

Before weekly topics, map:
- diagnostic evidence;
- formative checks;
- major summative tasks;
- cumulative/integrative evidence;
- independent assurance;
- feedback and revision;
- AI conditions.

Check workload and grading reliability.

## Step 4: Build learning progressions

For each capability:
- starting point;
- components;
- intermediate performances;
- integration;
- transfer;
- independence;
- success criteria.

## Step 5: Create module sequence

For each module:
- driving question;
- learning targets;
- prior retrieval;
- new model/method;
- guided practice;
- independent practice;
- formative evidence;
- feedback;
- cumulative connection;
- assessment contribution.

## Step 6: Map practice over time

Use a matrix with capability rows and weeks/modules columns:
- I = introduce;
- M = model;
- G = guided;
- P = practice;
- R = retrieve;
- V = vary/interleave;
- F = feedback;
- A = assess;
- T = transfer.

Look for one-and-done outcomes.

## Step 7: Design climate and access

Specify:
- welcome and disciplinary invitation;
- participation norms;
- help pathways;
- early success;
- accessible materials;
- flexible but coherent options;
- accommodation process;
- inclusive examples;
- recovery policies.

## Step 8: GenAI design

Create:
- course AI capability map;
- assignment-level rules;
- disclosure standard;
- privacy rules;
- independent assurance points;
- AI literacy instruction.

## Step 9: Workload

Estimate weekly student hours and peak weeks. Estimate marking. Remove or simplify low-value tasks.

## Step 10: Communication

Produce:
- course overview;
- outcomes;
- calendar/module map;
- assessment table;
- grading;
- policies;
- support;
- AI matrix;
- explanation of evidence-based routines.

## Step 11: Evaluation plan

Define:
- baseline;
- learning indicators;
- implementation indicators;
- workload;
- climate/access;
- disaggregation;
- midcourse feedback;
- revision decision.
