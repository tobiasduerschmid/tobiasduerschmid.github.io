# Lecture / Session Design Workflow

## Step 1: Define the session capability

Complete:
> By the end of the session, students can ______, using ______, under ______ conditions.

Identify one bottleneck and one likely misconception.

## Step 2: Define evidence

Choose an end-of-session performance:
- explain;
- solve;
- classify;
- interpret;
- decide;
- construct;
- critique;
- transfer.

Do not use attendance or “covered content” as evidence.

## Step 3: Activate

Design a 2–7 minute opener:
- retrieval;
- prediction;
- diagnostic question;
- case;
- comparison.

Use responses.

## Step 4: Explain/model

Decide:
- organizing question;
- structure;
- examples;
- representations;
- think-aloud;
- signaling;
- segmentation;
- load reduction.

## Step 5: Practice cycle

At least one:
- individual attempt;
- peer explanation;
- feedback;
- second attempt or transfer.

For novices, include worked example or scaffold.

## Step 6: Orchestrate

Specify:
- exact prompt;
- time;
- individual/group sequence;
- accountability;
- how responses are collected;
- what response patterns trigger;
- debrief.

## Step 7: Close

Use retrieval and synthesis:
- one-minute model;
- transfer item;
- error check;
- question;
- next connection.

## Step 8: Accessibility and logistics

Check:
- captions/alt text;
- readable materials;
- participation channels;
- room layout;
- technology fallback;
- time;
- AI rules.

## Step 9: Delayed return

Schedule:
- next-class retrieval;
- homework use;
- later mixed question;
- cumulative assessment.
