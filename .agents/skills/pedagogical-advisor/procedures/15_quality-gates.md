# Quality Gates

Apply checks proportionately to the requested deliverable. These are design-quality review statuses, not permission gates or reasons to leave useful reversible work unfinished. Do not require every technique in every lesson.

## Gate 1 — Purpose

- target capability is explicit;
- learner/context is defined;
- non-goals and constraints are clear.

## Gate 2 — Alignment

- evidence matches outcome;
- practice prepares the same cognitive work;
- criteria and grading represent the outcome;
- tools and collaboration do not invalidate inference.

## Gate 3 — Learning mechanism

- prior knowledge is activated/diagnosed;
- guidance fits expertise;
- cognitive load is managed;
- students perform meaningful cognitive work;
- important learning is retrieved and spaced;
- transfer is designed.

## Gate 4 — Feedback

- evidence appears before learning ends;
- feedback is actionable;
- revision or reattempt exists;
- teacher uses evidence.

## Gate 5 — Assessment quality

- claim, task, evidence, interpretation align;
- sampling is sufficient;
- scoring is dependable;
- fairness/access addressed;
- stakes match evidence quality.

## Gate 6 — Motivation and climate

- value and expectancy supported;
- challenge is achievable;
- help and recovery are credible;
- participation and belonging are considered.

## Gate 7 — Feasibility

- student time estimated;
- instructor/TA workload estimated;
- technology and room logistics work;
- fallback exists;
- design can be sustained.

## Gate 8 — Accessibility

- materials accessible;
- irrelevant barriers reduced;
- accommodations supported;
- options preserve construct.

## Gate 9 — GenAI

- AI role explicit;
- productive effort preserved;
- disclosure/verification/privacy defined;
- independent assurance included if needed;
- no detector dependence.

## Gate 10 — Evaluation

- implementation indicators;
- learning indicators;
- burden/access indicators;
- disaggregation;
- decision for revision.

## Status

- **PASS:** ready.
- **PASS WITH CONDITIONS:** usable if stated conditions are met.
- **REVISE:** important gaps.
- **STOP:** invalid, unfair, unsafe, or infeasible.

Report only the most consequential unresolved gates.

## Artifact and evidence checks added in version 2

- Review actual content and preserve explicit template/structure constraints; do not imply unseen pages were inspected.
- Verify technical content and keys separately from learning design. State execution/rendering limits honestly.
- A claim about individual learning needs the relevant observation conditions; banning AI in policy does not itself provide independent evidence.
- Source versions, measured outcomes, access limits, and original design synthesis are explicit where consequential.
- Simulated students and agent review consensus are not observations of learner outcomes.
- Structural lint and editorial rubric totals are not validated scores of teaching effectiveness.
