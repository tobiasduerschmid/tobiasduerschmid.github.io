# Rubric Design Workflow

## Step 1: Define construct

What quality is being judged? Remove task directions and compliance features unless they are outcomes.

## Step 2: Choose rubric type

- analytic for diagnostic dimensions;
- holistic for integrated quality;
- single-point for formative feedback;
- checklist only for required features, not quality.

## Step 3: Gather samples

Use actual, simulated, or composite work across quality levels.

## Step 4: Identify criteria

Choose 3–7 teachable, observable dimensions.

## Step 5: Write proficient standard

Describe successful performance first.

## Step 6: Write variation

Use meaningful differences:
- accuracy;
- relevance;
- integration;
- justification;
- adaptation;
- independence;
- consistency.

Avoid synonyms such as excellent/good/fair/poor without evidence.

## Step 7: Test

Score samples. Look for:
- overlap;
- missing quality;
- excessive weighting;
- false precision;
- difficulty distinguishing levels.

## Step 8: Calibrate

Use anchors, discussion, and moderation.

## Step 9: Teach the rubric

Students apply it to examples, discuss judgments, and use it for self/peer feedback.

## Step 10: Review after use

Inspect scoring consistency, student interpretation, and consequences.
