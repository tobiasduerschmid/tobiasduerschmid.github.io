# GenAI-Aware Redesign Workflow

## Step 1: Identify the threatened inference

What claim becomes uncertain when GenAI can produce the output?

Example:
- report quality no longer shows independent analysis;
- code no longer shows decomposition skill;
- reflection no longer shows personal monitoring.

## Step 2: Classify capability

- unaided;
- AI-augmented;
- AI-evaluative;
- AI-design;
- relational/ethical.

## Step 3: Decide AI role

For each stage:
- prohibit;
- permit;
- require;
- constrain as tutor/hints;
- use as object of critique.

## Step 4: Preserve cognitive work

Require:
- initial attempt;
- prediction;
- explanation;
- verification;
- comparison;
- revision;
- retrieval;
- unaided demonstration.

## Step 5: Redesign evidence

Combine:
- authentic AI-integrated task;
- process artifacts;
- oral defense;
- live or controlled sample;
- individual component;
- cumulative evidence.

## Step 6: Policy

State:
- tools;
- uses;
- disclosure;
- citation;
- privacy;
- verification;
- responsibility;
- consequences.

## Step 7: Equity

Provide access or alternatives. Avoid premium-model advantage.

## Step 8: Teach AI literacy

Include model limitations, source checking, bias, privacy, and appropriate reliance.

## Step 9: Assurance map

Across the course/program, map where each outcome has credible evidence. Do not place all assurance in one event.

## Step 10: Evaluate

Collect:
- supported performance;
- later unaided performance;
- reliance patterns;
- error detection;
- student process;
- workload;
- equity.

Run `rubrics/genai-resilience-rubric.md`.
