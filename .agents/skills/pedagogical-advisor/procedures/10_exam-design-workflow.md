# Exam Design Workflow

## Step 1: Define exam purpose

- diagnostic, practice, midterm, final, certification;
- stakes and consequences;
- closed/open resource;
- supervised/take-home;
- independent/tool-mediated.

## Step 2: Blueprint

Allocate:
- outcomes;
- content;
- cognitive demand;
- item formats;
- marks;
- time;
- difficulty;
- transfer;
- cumulative content.

Do not draft items first.

## Step 3: Item specifications

For each item:
- outcome;
- evidence;
- format;
- prompt;
- key/rubric;
- common errors/distractors;
- difficulty;
- time;
- accessibility;
- AI/security note.

## Step 4: Draft

Follow format-specific guidelines. Avoid tricks, irrelevant reading load, and cueing.

## Step 5: Independent review

Have another knowledgeable person:
- solve;
- time;
- identify ambiguity;
- map to blueprint;
- check accuracy and bias.

## Step 6: Scoring

Prepare:
- key;
- partial credit;
- rubric;
- alternative answers;
- moderation;
- marker training.

## Step 7: Administration

Specify:
- instructions;
- allowed materials;
- accommodations;
- technology fallback;
- integrity;
- question handling;
- extra-time logistics.

## Step 8: Student preparation

Provide:
- outcome scope;
- format;
- practice items;
- standards;
- retrieval plan;
- not exact replicas.

## Step 9: Post-exam review

Analyze:
- item functioning;
- timing;
- reliability/dependability;
- common errors;
- subgroup patterns;
- teaching implications.

Correct flawed items transparently.

## Step 10: Learning after exam

Use:
- corrections;
- wrapper;
- reattempt;
- cumulative return.

Run `rubrics/exam-design-rubric.md`.
