# Knowledge Map

The knowledge base is organized from foundational mechanisms to design applications. Load the smallest useful set.

## Foundation

- `01_learning-model.md` — an integrated model of learning: prior knowledge, attention, working memory, knowledge organization, practice, feedback, motivation, metacognition, culture, and transfer.
- `02_evidence-reasoning.md` — how to interpret education research, evidence strength, effect sizes, heterogeneity, and causal claims.

## Design architecture

- `03_course-design.md` — course-level design, sequencing, module coherence, workload, and course climate.
- `04_learning-objectives-and-alignment.md` — capability statements, evidence, constructive alignment, learning progressions, and assessment claims.
- `05_lecture-and-session-design.md` — design of explanations, demonstrations, questioning, practice, and closing synthesis.
- `06_active-learning.md` — cognitively meaningful active learning, ICAP, peer instruction, group design, and large-class implementation.
- `07_practice-memory-and-mastery.md` — retrieval, spacing, interleaving, generation, deliberate practice, fluency, and cumulative design.
- `08_cognitive-load-and-multimedia.md` — working-memory limits, worked examples, fading, signaling, redundancy, modality, and expertise reversal.
- `09_transfer-expertise-and-authenticity.md` — expert knowledge, transfer, analogies, varied contexts, authentic practice, and adaptive expertise.

## Assessment

- `10_assessment-foundations.md` — assessment purpose, the assessment triangle, evidence-centered design, validity, reliability, fairness, and authenticity.
- `11_formative-assessment-and-feedback.md` — diagnostic/formative systems, feedback loops, revision, peer/self-assessment, and exam wrappers.
- `12_exam-design-and-psychometrics.md` — exam blueprints, item formats, cognitive demand, scoring, quality review, security, and post-exam analysis.
- `13_homework-and-assignment-design.md` — purposeful homework, problem sets, writing, projects, workload, feedback, and academic integrity.
- `17_grading-rubrics-and-academic-judgment.md` — criteria, standards, analytic/holistic rubrics, calibration, grading architecture, and judgment.

## Learner and environment

- `14_motivation-belonging-and-climate.md` — expectancy, value, goals, autonomy, competence, interest, belonging, anxiety, and classroom climate.
- `15_accessibility-inclusion-and-udl.md` — proactive barrier reduction, UDL 3.0, accommodations, language, accessibility, and construct relevance.
- `16_collaboration-discussion-and-pbl.md` — structured group learning, discussion, argumentation, project/problem-based learning, and facilitation.
- `20_metacognition-and-self-regulated-learning.md` — planning, monitoring, calibration, strategy instruction, reflection, and learner agency.
- `21_discipline-and-level-adaptation.md` — novice/advanced differences and adaptation across STEM, humanities, professional, lab, studio, and seminar contexts.

## Technology and improvement

- `18_online-hybrid-and-technology.md` — online/hybrid presence, interaction, media, practice, feedback, analytics, and technology selection.
- `19_genai-in-teaching-and-assessment.md` — learning-preserving GenAI use, policy, process evidence, disclosure, verification, and assessment reform.
- `22_course-evaluation-and-sotl.md` — local evidence, rapid improvement cycles, course evaluation, peer review, and scholarship of teaching and learning.
- `23_myths-controversies-and-boundaries.md` — myths, contested findings, false dichotomies, and limits of generalization.
