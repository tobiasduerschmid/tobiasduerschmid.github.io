# Assessment Design Rubric

Score 0–4. Apply more stringent evidence expectations as stakes rise.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Purpose/decision | Unknown | Mixed or implicit | General purpose stated | Decision and stakes explicit | Uses and limits of score interpretations are explicit |
| Construct/claim | Product or points only | Vague skill label | Outcome identified | Capability, context, and standard specified | Cognitive model/progression explains what development looks like |
| Observation/task | Does not elicit target | Weak proxy | Partially aligned | Task elicits relevant performance | Multiple complementary observations reduce construct underrepresentation |
| Interpretation/criteria | No defensible rule | Vague judgment | Criteria exist | Observable criteria and scoring rules align | Exemplars, calibration, and boundary rules support defensible judgment |
| Validity threats | Ignored | Obvious confounds | Some barriers reviewed | Alternative explanations and irrelevant variance mitigated | Evidence is accumulated and interpretation limits are documented |
| Reliability/precision | Arbitrary | Single noisy sample | Basic consistency | Sampling/scoring adequate for stakes | Precision, marker agreement, and uncertainty inform decisions |
| Fairness/access | Exclusionary | Reactive accommodation | Basic accessibility | Construct-preserving access and bias review | Differential functioning/experience is monitored and corrected |
| Authenticity/transfer | Decorative context | Familiar reproduction | Some application | Meaningful disciplinary/professional performance | Authenticity plus controlled variation supports transfer inference |
| Feedback/learning use | Judgment only | Score/comment | Some usable feedback | Timely feedback and required revision/correction | Assessment develops evaluative judgment and informs teaching systemically |
| Workload/feasibility | Impossible | Hidden burden | Roughly manageable | Piloted/estimated and sustainable | Scalable process with calibration, technology fallback, and moderation |
| GenAI/assurance | Ambiguous/detector reliance | Blanket rule | Basic policy | AI conditions align to construct; process/unaided evidence where needed | Assessment develops AI-era capability while triangulating independent learning |
| Post-use review | None | Anecdotal | Basic performance review | Item/task, error, fairness, and workload evidence reviewed | Documented retain/revise/retire decisions and longitudinal evidence |

## Red flags
- A polished product is accepted as proof of student capability without process or independent evidence.
- A criterion rewards surface features more than the intended construct.
- Speed, language, technology, or cultural familiarity drives scores without being part of the outcome.
- A single low-reliability score supports a high-consequence decision.
