# Exam Design and Review Rubric

Score 0–3.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blueprint | Items drafted ad hoc | Content list only | Outcome × demand plan | Weighted blueprint guides item count, points, and time |
| Sampling | Major outcomes omitted | One item per major claim | Adequate breadth | Multiple independent samples proportional to importance |
| Cognitive demand | Mostly recall despite higher goals | Labels overstate demand | Appropriate mixture | Reasoning, application, discrimination, and transfer are authentically elicited |
| Item quality | Ambiguous/inaccurate/clued | Several flaws | Mostly clear and reviewed | Second review, pilot/history, and misconception-based distractors |
| Timing | Unknown or speeded accidentally | Instructor estimate only | Plausible with buffer | Piloted/empirical timing; speed justified if assessed |
| Scoring | Inconsistent or post hoc | Minimal key | Detailed key/partial credit | Calibration, moderation, boundary rules, and uncertainty handled |
| Reliability | Ignored | Assumed | Basic internal/marker evidence | Reliability/precision interpreted in light of use and sampling |
| Validity | Score treated as self-validating | Content match only | Claim-task alignment reviewed | Alternative explanations, underrepresentation, and consequences examined |
| Fairness/access | Known barriers | generic accommodations | accessible format and review | bias/sensitivity review and construct-preserving arrangements |
| Integrity/AI | security theater/detector | blanket controls | conditions explicit | proportionate security plus independent assurance and fallback |
| Feedback | grade only | answer key | review/corrections | error analysis, wrapper/retrieval, and instructional response |
| Post-exam governance | items reused blindly | anecdotal edits | statistics/qualitative review | documented item-bank decision with exposure and fairness history |

An exam cannot compensate for a course in which students never practised the assessed demand.
