# Lecture and Class-Session Design Rubric

Score 0–3 per dimension.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Target | No session-level learning target | Topic stated | Observable target but broad | One/few precise capabilities linked to course progression |
| Prior knowledge | No activation/diagnosis | Generic recap | Relevant retrieval | Retrieval exposes misconception and changes support |
| Coherence | Content dump | Some headings | Clear model/question and signaling | Explanation is selective, integrated, and revisited through application |
| Cognitive load | Redundant, transient, split, or overloaded | Some avoidable load | Mostly segmented/signaled | Load is deliberately managed by expertise, representations, and pacing |
| Modeling | Final answer only | Demonstration without reasoning | Worked example or think-aloud | Decisions, standards, errors, and fading are explicit |
| Student cognitive work | Passive throughout | Participation without clear thinking | Students retrieve/apply/explain | Constructive/interactive work is required and aligned throughout |
| Formative evidence | No all-student evidence | Volunteer questions only | Hinge/response collected | Evidence maps to misconceptions and triggers planned action |
| Feedback | None or delayed | Correct answer only | Explanation and correction | Students revise, revote, or reapply feedback in-session |
| Participation/access | One narrow participation route | Informal inclusion | Thinking time and accessible materials | Multiple equivalent routes, structured voice, and contingency planning |
| Motivation/climate | Threat, opacity, or trivia | Relevance asserted | Value and attainable challenge | Authentic purpose, autonomy within structure, and error-friendly norms |
| Cumulative learning | Session isolated | Summary only | Post-class practice | Retrieves earlier learning and schedules later retrieval/transfer |
| Feasibility | Timing/logistics implausible | Fragile | Workable | Timed run-of-show, cut points, room/tech fallback, staff roles |

**Strong threshold:** no dimension below 2 and at least six dimensions at 3.
