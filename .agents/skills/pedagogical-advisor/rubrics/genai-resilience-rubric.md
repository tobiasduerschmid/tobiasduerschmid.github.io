# GenAI-Resilient Learning Design Rubric

“Resilient” means the design preserves learning and supports defensible claims as tools improve. It does not mean an assignment is permanently impossible for AI.

Score 0–4.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Learning target | Product completion mistaken for learning | Target implied | Underlying capability named | Unaided and AI-enabled capabilities separated | Course progression explicitly develops both and their relationship |
| Productive effort | AI can remove all target work | Ban is only safeguard | Some learner work retained | Attempt, retrieval, explanation, verification, and revision are designed | Assistance adapts while preserving challenge and transfer |
| Evidence architecture | Final product only | superficial process log | process or oral check | staged artifacts + reasoning + independent evidence | triangulated longitudinal evidence with proportionate assurance |
| AI rules | absent/contradictory | blanket ban/permission | task-specific status | phase/function-specific rules with rationale | rules are teachable, consistent, reviewed as tools change |
| Verification | output accepted | “check it” instruction | sources/calculations checked | explicit verification protocol and accountability | students compare methods, uncertainty, provenance, and consequences |
| Disclosure/provenance | none | generic citation request | tool/use disclosed | inputs, outputs, decisions, and modifications documented proportionately | provenance supports reflection without creating surveillance burden |
| Evaluative judgment | AI treated as authority | error spotting only | critique required | criteria-driven comparison and revision | students decide when not to use AI and justify tradeoffs |
| Independent assurance | none | high-stakes final only | one controlled check | distributed low/medium-stakes demonstrations | assurance is sampled across capability, time, and context |
| Access/privacy | commercial account assumed | opt-out unclear | basic alternative | supported tool, privacy rules, equivalent pathway | accessibility, data governance, cost, and tool failure are integrated |
| Robustness to improvement | depends on current model weakness | obscurity/“AI-proofing” | local/contextual task | authentic decisions and evidence survive better models | periodic adversarial testing and redesign process documented |
| Workload/feasibility | impossible surveillance | heavy manual policing | manageable | process evidence is sampled/automated ethically | sustainable at scale with clear escalation and student support |
| Learning evaluation | misconduct counts only | perceptions only | product quality | unaided learning and tool-use quality measured | causal/longitudinal evidence informs policy and task revision |

## Automatic failures
- AI-detector probability is treated as proof of authorship or misconduct.
- Students are required to upload confidential, personal, proprietary, or restricted data to an external model.
- AI is required without an accessible and financially equivalent pathway.
- A fluent AI-assisted artifact is used as the sole basis for certifying individual competence.
