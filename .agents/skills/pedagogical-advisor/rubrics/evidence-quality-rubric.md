# Evidence Quality and Claim Calibration Rubric

Use this before calling a teaching practice “proven” or “best.” Score 0–3 on each dimension.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Source authority | opinion/marketing | secondary summary | peer-reviewed or official | systematic review/meta-analysis or strong primary field evidence, appropriately used |
| Design strength | anecdote | correlational/pre-post only | quasi-experimental or strong qualitative causal account | randomized/replicated or convergent designs suited to the question |
| Construct validity | unclear outcome | proxy with major limits | reasonable measure | measure matches intended learning and interpretation |
| Generalizability | one narrow setting treated universally | context omitted | context described | moderators, population, domain, dosage, and implementation boundaries explicit |
| Consistency | cherry-picked | mixed evidence ignored | conflict acknowledged | convergence and heterogeneity synthesized, including null/negative findings |
| Magnitude/precision | significance only | effect without uncertainty | magnitude and uncertainty | educational importance, baseline risk, precision, and costs interpreted |
| Mechanism | slogan | plausible story | evidence-linked mechanism | mechanism informs implementation and predicts failure modes |
| Recency/relevance | obsolete for current claim | date unchecked | current enough | updated search for changing topics and primary guidance |
| Bias/conflicts | ignored | limited review | major biases noted | publication bias, study quality, conflicts, and selective reporting considered |
| Actionability | recipe detached from evidence | broad recommendation | conditions stated | recommendation, implementation, burden, and local evaluation are linked |

## Evidence labels
- **A:** replicated/convergent and generally robust, with stated conditions.
- **B:** supported but materially conditional or implementation-dependent.
- **C:** promising/emerging; pilot and evaluate.
- **D:** contested/insufficient; present alternatives and uncertainty.
- **X:** disconfirmed or commonly misapplied.

A strong meta-analysis does not make every implementation of a broad label effective. The unit of advice must be specific enough to reproduce the mechanism.
