## Reconciliation rules that change teaching advice

1. **Resolve the database-course conflict.** For [23], use SIG25-02's inspected full-text analysis. A lower mean without a significant difference is not an established learning deficit; voluntary AI use remains confounded. Compare the assisted assignment component with an appropriately independent outcome, and report both uncertainty and subgroup differences.
2. **Do not count the same learners twice.** [43] is a secondary analysis of the 33 AI-arm learners in [6]. Link its behavioral interpretation to that cohort, not a new replication. Reviews [2–5, 32–34, 37–39] may overlap with one another and with individual reports; no pooled total N or study-vote calculation is supported here. Similar tools or author teams do not prove independence or duplication: [12]/[25] require dataset checking before combined inference.
3. **Protect outcome distinctions in materials.** [25] lost its intended posttest to time constraints; [42] measures dialogue, and [26] concerns assisted artifacts. They can improve activity design without serving as proof of unaided learning. Build explicit time for an independent application instead of using engagement as a substitute.
4. **Preserve comparator and intervention.** [35] compares adaptive with fixed sequencing under the same AI tutor; [31] augments a human tutor; [30] is non-generative assistance. These cannot establish the effect of replacing teaching with a general chatbot.
5. **Keep publication year separate from evidence date and track.** [5] is published in 2025 within ITiCSE 2024 working-group reports; [25] is a SIGCSE 2025 poster; [19–20] are ICER, not SIGCSE TS. [38] searches only through October 2024, despite a 2026 publication. Retain [31,35,36] working-paper/preprint status and final-versus-author-version differences.
6. **Teach explanation with a check.** [17]/[42] motivate students teaching a questioning agent; [32] motivates better help-seeking decisions. Require a subsequent explanation, correction or changed problem that demonstrates the target capability. Interaction length, productive-looking dialogue and reduced frustration are legitimate process outcomes, but cannot certify mastery.
7. **Keep retraction and uncertainty visible.** [40] excludes one specific retracted meta-analysis; it does not invalidate every positive AI study. [37]'s heterogeneous and bias-sensitive average cannot be used to forecast a student's gain or claim that AI never helps. Do not import effect estimates repeated in another paper's introduction without checking the original status.

## Access and review-method limits to preserve

The supplied package describes a bounded systematic search and critical narrative synthesis, not a preregistered exhaustive review with independent human dual screening. It reports Sider Scholar/OpenAlex, SciSpace and complementary web work; Elicit API access was denied and Consensus quota was exhausted. Do not claim those unavailable services supplied evidence, or that this skill update reran the review's searches. The initial discovery and citation-following counts are search records, not independent learning studies or an exhaustive PRISMA flow.

The original review has several abstract-only or metadata-only sources, marked above and in its access checklist. Do not fill missing randomization, assistance status, attrition, retention interval, effect size or final-version details from a title or secondary summary. For consequential teaching changes, retrieve the relevant primary sections or use a small local pilot whose outcome and limitations are explicit. The directly studied contexts remain concentrated in introductory programming and short tasks; extrapolation to requirements engineering, architecture, security, testing and large team projects must remain labeled as design inference.
